How to Play

  • 📌
    Pull the Pins: Click or drag pins to remove them and release fluids.
  • 💧
    Water is Life: Guide fresh blue water into the fish's bowl so it can breathe and swim!
  • 🌋
    Danger - Lava: Lava will burn the fish! Mix water with lava to turn it into harmless solid stone.
  • 🦀
    Crabs & Enemies: Eliminate dangerous creatures with lava or heavy falling rocks!
