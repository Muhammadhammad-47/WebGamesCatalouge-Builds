How to Play

+=
1. Merge petsDrag a pet onto a matching pet to merge them into a new, higher-level pet. There are 13 to discover.
🪙
2. Earn coinsEvery pet earns coins by itself. Tap a pet for a quick bonus.
🏠
3. Fill adoption ordersDrag the pet a family wants onto their card on the Adoption Board for big coin and XP rewards.
🥚
4. Get more petsTap Buy egg, or wait for the free egg bar to fill.
🔓
5. Grow your yardTap a tile that shows a price to unlock more space.
🛍️
6. Shop & SellTap Shop to buy upgrades. Tap Sell, then tap a pet to sell it (or drag a pet onto Sell).

Keyboard: Space buys an egg · F full screen · Esc back
