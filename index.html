How to Play
1

Numbered Star Nodes

Each starting node displays a number. This number is the exact total length in grid cells that its line must cover (including the starting node).

2

Drag to Draw Paths

Click/touch a numbered node and drag horizontally or vertically across adjacent cells to extend its glowing path.

3

Retract or Undo

Drag backwards to retract a path, click an intermediate point on a path to trim it, or tap Undo to step back.

4

Complete Coverage

Paths cannot overlap or intersect. To complete a constellation, every line must reach its exact length and 100% of all playable tiles must be covered!
