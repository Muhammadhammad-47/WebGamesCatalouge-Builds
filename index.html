❓ How to Play

  • Rotate & Zoom: Drag anywhere to spin the 3D knitted model; scroll or pinch to zoom in.
  • Tap to Unravel: Tap outer yarn pieces (not blocked by outer layers) to wind them into matching color baskets.
  • Match 3 Spools: Fill a basket with 3 matching spools to clear it and reveal a new basket!
  • Press & Hold to Peek: Hold on any outer yarn piece to peek at the hidden layer underneath.
  • Manage Spare Holes: Don't let all 5 spare holes fill up! Use Shuffle, +1 Hole, or Backup Jar if needed.
