How to Play

  1. Splotch rolls on its own. Tap (or press Space) to flip gravity between the floor and the ceiling.
  2. Every tile you roll over gets painted. Paint as much of the tunnel as you can!
  3. Gaps and spikes are deadly. Flip to the other side to get past them.
  4. Grab the 3 paint drops 💧 in each level for 3 stars.
