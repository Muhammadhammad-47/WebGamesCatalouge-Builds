# Pet Merge Haven

An original merge-and-adopt pet game built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. All art is drawn procedurally on canvas (no external images).

## How to play
- Drag two identical pets together to merge them into a new, higher-level pet (13 pets to discover).
- Every pet earns coins over time; tap a pet for a bonus.
- Drag pets onto **families waiting to adopt** to fulfil their orders for big rewards.
- Buy eggs, or wait for the free egg bar. Drop unwanted pets on **Sell**.
- Spend coins in the **Shop** on upgrades. Progress is saved in your browser.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.
